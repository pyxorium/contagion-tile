Contagion
This page doesn't save Contagion games.

Object of the game

Keep the contamination contained while protecting resources.

How to play

Roll all the dice. 🦠 contamination is always set aside and counted.

Then keep one type showing: ⚗️ defense (as often as you like), or 🏠 home, 🏥 hospital or 🚰 water (each only once a game). Every die of that type is set aside.

Then roll the dice that are left again, or bank.

The game ends when you bank, when the grid is empty, or when a roll leaves nothing you can keep.

Score

Contained (⚗️ at least as many as 🦠): one point per resource, +3 for all three types, +1 for emptying the grid; at least 1.

Not contained: 0. If a roll leaves nothing to keep: 1 if contained, otherwise 0.

Daily: today's dice are the same for everyone. Free play: new dice every game.
